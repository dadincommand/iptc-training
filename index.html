Phase 1: Comms, Intel & Scripts Mandatory Setup

Open Discord Invite

War calls, chain watches, and faction announcements run through Discord.

  1. Click Open Discord Invite and accept the server invite.
  2. Run the verification command in the welcome channel so your server nickname matches your Torn Name [ID].
  3. Enable notifications for war & chain ping channels.
Open TornStats

Used for battle stat tracking, spy sharing, and organized crime readiness.

  1. Go to tornstats.com and log in using a Limited or Full Torn API key.
  2. Navigate to Faction in the left menu and confirm you are linked to Infernum Perdition.
  3. Check that your gym gains and stat history sync properly.
Open YATA

YATA handles faction war target lists, armory tracking, and travel stock intel.

  1. Visit yata.yt and sign in with your Torn API key.
  2. Open the Faction tab to verify your membership status syncs.
  3. Keep your key active so your target availability and travel data update during wars.
Get CAT Script

CAT is our core faction combat & travel userscript. Install it on desktop or mobile:

  1. Desktop (Chrome/Firefox/Edge): Install the Tampermonkey browser extension, then click Get CAT Script and hit Install.
  2. Mobile (TornPDA): Open Settings → Advanced Browser Settings → Manage Scripts, tap +, and paste the CAT script URL or source code.
  3. Open Torn, open CAT's Settings, and verify your preferred modules:
Fast Attack: One-button fight & finish (Leave/Mug/Hosp)
Auto Call: Auto-claims uncalled war targets
Travel Assist: Best trip picker, auto-pack & restock alerts
Attack Page: Enemy weapon bonuses & loadout display
Table Tools: Online/Idle/Offline counter & Dot Row Style
Always-On Links: Clickable Attack links in hosp/jail/abroad
